Every figure below comes from the Income Tax Act 2023 as amended by the Finance Act 2026 — slab rates, the salary one-third exemption, house property allowances, the investment rebate test, minimum tax and the net-worth surcharge. Nothing is sent anywhere; the whole calculation runs in this page.

House propertyগৃহসম্পত্তি

A flat repair-and-maintenance allowance replaces receipts: 25% of annual value for residential lettings, 30% for commercial. Municipal tax, land revenue, insurance and housing-loan interest come off on top.

These sit outside the slab table. For an ordinary shareholder, gains on listed securities are exempt up to ৳50,00,000 in a year and the excess is taxed at 15% — sponsor, director and placement shares do not get that exemption.

The rebate is the lowest of three numbers: 3% of taxable income, 10% of what you actually invested, and ৳7,50,000. Only the schedule-listed investments count.
