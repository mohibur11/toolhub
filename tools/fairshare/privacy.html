FairShare

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

FairShare is a free tool for tracking shared expenses within groups of friends. It is a personal, non-commercial project published on Toolhub. This page explains what data FairShare handles and how.

How FairShare works

FairShare has no server. The app runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to Google with your own Google sign-in. Nothing you enter is sent to the app's author or to anyone else, and the author has no access to your groups.

Google permissions it asks for

  • Your name, email address and profile photo, to show who added what in a group.
  • Google Drive, only the files FairShare creates or that you open with it (drive.file). This is how FairShare creates a group's Google Sheet and opens groups shared with you after you select them in Google's file picker. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Its own hidden settings file in your Drive (drive.appdata), which keeps your list of groups and the name you chose in FairShare, so they follow you across devices. Only FairShare can read it.

FairShare cannot read your Gmail, contacts or any other Google data.

Where your data is stored

  • Each group is a Google Sheet in its creator's Google Drive (the group owner), shared with edit access with the group's members. It holds the members' emails, names and profile photo links, the expenses (descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, notes, how they are split), payments between members, and an activity history.
  • Your list of groups and your FairShare name are in the hidden settings file in your own Drive.

Google stores these files under its own privacy policy and terms.

Who can see your data

  • Members of a group can see everything in that group, in the app and in its Google Sheet (they have edit access to the sheet, as in any shared Google file).
  • The group owner owns the sheet in their Drive.
  • Nobody else. FairShare has no server and no admin account with access to your groups.

Limited Use

FairShare's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide FairShare's features to you, is never sold, never used for advertising, and never transferred to anyone except as needed to share a group with the members you choose.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics scripts, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Data on your device

FairShare keeps your Google access (which expires after about an hour), your basic profile and a copy of your group list in your browser's local storage, so it starts quickly. Signing out removes them. The app itself may also be cached on your device so it loads quickly.

Retention and deletion

  • A group's owner can delete the group from its settings. Its sheet goes to the owner's Google Drive trash, which Google empties after 30 days, and members lose access.
  • Any member with a settled balance can leave a group; they are marked as having left and lose access to its sheet.
  • You can remove FairShare's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and delete its hidden settings file in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → FairShare → Delete hidden app data.
  • Group sheets you own stay in your Drive like any other file, until you or the app deletes them.

Children

FairShare is not directed at children under 13.
