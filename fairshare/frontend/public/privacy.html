FairShare

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

FairShare is a free tool for tracking shared expenses within groups of friends. It is a personal, non-commercial project published on Toolhub. This page explains what data FairShare handles and how.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. FairShare requests no other Google permissions. It cannot read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What you and your group members enter: group names, member email addresses, expenses (descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, notes, how they are split), payments between members and optional receipt photos.
  • Activity history: a log of who added, edited or deleted what in each group, so members can see changes.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive belonging to the Google account that runs FairShare (the "owner account"), and is processed by a Google Apps Script program running under that account. Google's own privacy policy applies to that storage.

Who can see your data

  • Members of your group can see that group's members, expenses, payments, receipts and activity in the app.
  • The owner account that runs FairShare can technically access all stored data.
  • People outside your group cannot see your group's data in the app.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics scripts, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • Your data is used only to provide FairShare's features.

Data on your device

FairShare keeps a sign-in session token and your basic profile in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in (sessions last up to 30 days). Signing out removes them. The app may also be cached on your device so it loads quickly.

Retention and deletion

  • A group's owner can permanently delete the group from its settings. This removes its expenses, payments and receipts.
  • Deleted files are purged from Google Drive trash within 30 days.
  • Weekly backups are kept for about 8 weeks, so deleted data fully disappears within that time.
  • Any member with a settled balance can leave a group.
  • To have your account details (name, email, photo) removed, contact us using the link below.

Children

FairShare is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
